## Deviations and ambiguities (kept, best for the game)
- Roll cooldown counts from the end of the roll, so a chained roll is never shorter than 1 s.
- Boss bar sits at bottom centre, not the top of the HUD (it does not overlap the hearts).
- Marshal Grimm takes 0.85x damage (design decision, keeps the fight length).
- A shop pedestal may hold an active item, although the spec says passive.
- Powder keg does 80 damage at radius 320, versus "3 dmg" in the spec text. Dynamite does 60 to enemies.
- Enemy bullets don't damage breakables.
- Menu reads "RIDE OUT" with ENTER, not the literal "Press ENTER to start".
- Run timer under-counts at very low fps because dt is clamped.
- Cause of death for generic enemy bullets shows "A stray bullet".
- Best time is recorded for wins only.
- Secret rooms can be adjacent to the start room.

## Not verified
- Bullet readability on floors 2/3 was not re-checked visually.
- Dynamite damage to enemies and breakables was verified in code only, not in a running-game screenshot.
